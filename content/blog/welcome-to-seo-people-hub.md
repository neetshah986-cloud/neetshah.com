I'm Neet Shah, an SEO specialist, and SEO People Hub is my free set of tools and guides for people who do SEO for a living, or who are learning to.

## What's coming first

- **Page Speed Test**: Core Web Vitals and Lighthouse results for any URL, with fixes in priority order.
- **Schema Validator**: every structured data item on a page, checked for errors and rich result eligibility.
- **SSR vs CSR Checker**: a side-by-side view of the HTML a crawler sees before JavaScript runs and the fully rendered page.

More tools for meta tags, robots.txt and redirect chains will follow.

## Why rendering matters

Search engines can render JavaScript, but rendering is queued and costly. Content, links and meta tags that only appear after client-side rendering can be discovered late or missed. Being able to see the difference quickly is one of the most useful checks in technical SEO, and it's why the SSR vs CSR Checker is one of the first tools on the list.
